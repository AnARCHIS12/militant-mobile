Militant is the decentralized social network built for activists, grassroots groups, unions, and committed campaigners.

Take back control of your communication with a free, open platform—free of algorithmic manipulation, advertisements, and tracking.

Key Features:
• Real-time chronological feed: Never miss an update from your comrades.
• Communities and Groups: Organize and join local or thematic action groups.
• Secure Instant Messaging: Chat in direct messages or group discussions with confidence.
• Fediverse & ActivityPub: Connect seamlessly with the broader decentralized network.
• Free Push Notifications (ntfy): Real-time alerts powered by self-hosted, de-Googled push.
• Media Sharing: Share photos, videos, and documents without algorithmic suppression.
• Activist Events: Coordinate and join local mobilizations and collective actions.

100% Free and Open Source software for liberated communication.
